# Route a flex board between two connectors

A flex board that joins two boards carries one bus between two board-to-board connectors. Read the knowledge entries `layout-flex-bend-and-stiffener` and `layout-flex-connector-bus` first, and the team's flex and routing rules that `xpedition-cli knowledge list --source team --applies-to trace create` returns: they decide the fan-out, the clearances, the via limits and where vias may go.

1. Read the board. `pcb get` gives the outline, the stiffeners (`keepout_kinds.height_limits`), the bend zones (`bend_zones`, each with what marks it), the plane shapes (`planes`), the cutouts, the net classes and the via padstacks; `pcb get --output <file>` adds every pad, keepout and cutout shape. `component get --refdes <connector>` gives each pin's net and position, which shows the two rows and the pins that match from one end to the other. When the board marks no bend zone, ask the person where it bends.
2. Set the rules. Give the power and speaker nets classes as wide as their current needs, define the differential pairs and the spacing between the two nets of a pair, then `constraint import --file <rules.json>` ([rules-file.md](rules-file.md)).
3. Plan the lanes. Write one routing plan for `trace create` ([routing-plan.md](routing-plan.md)):
   - Give every net a lane under `lanes`: its `offset_mm` from one board edge, the edge named by two points of `edge_mm`, and its fan-out at each end in `from_mm` and `to_mm`. The tool follows the outline with it, so lanes at offsets one pitch apart (width plus clearance) stay evenly spaced through every turn, and a pair's two lanes come out nearly the same length.
   - At each connector, fan the traces out from the pins to their lanes inside the stiffener: the row nearer the board's body on the pad layer, the other row through one via beside each pin. A connector on the bottom side has its pads on the bottom layer, so a net that leaves the other end on the top layer changes layers beside the pin. A via takes a padstack the design uses (`pcb get` `via_padstacks`) or one of the central library (`padstack list --doc <project> --type via`); `padstack create` adds one. Finish every turn and width change before a bend zone, and cross the zone straight.
   - Give each ground pin a via beside it inside the stiffener.
   - Run `trace create --file <plan.json> --dry-run`: the preview gives each pair's two lengths and what the bend zones forbid of the plan, and lists every width or clearance it breaks. Change the plan until it shows none.
4. Draw. `trace create --file <plan.json>` refuses a plan that breaks a width or a clearance and lists every place; change the plan and run it again. Its result says for each net whether it is routed.
5. Pour. `plane create --net GND --layer <n>` for each layer; for a bend zone, pour the solid part and the hatched band as separate shapes with `--from-y` and `--to-y`, the band with `--hatch diagonal --hatch-width --hatch-distance`. A power net carried on two layers gets a copper area at each end on each layer, `plane create --net <power> --layer <n> --polygon <points>` over its pins and lanes inside the stiffener, joined through the layers by vias inside it (`via create --net <power> --x --y`), as many as its current needs. A copper area gets copper only once a pad, trace or via of its net touches it on its layer: read `generated` in `pcb get` `planes`.
6. Stitch. `via create --net GND --pitch <distance>` with `--from-y` and `--to-y` (or `--from-x` and `--to-x`) places a grid of ground stitching vias between two bend zones and none inside them; `via create --net GND --x --y` places one.
7. Check. `pcb check` lists the hazards and the board rules: on a flex board what each bend zone and stiffener forbids, and `pair_skew` for a pair whose two lengths differ by more than 0.127 mm. `pcb get` must report no open net, and its `metrics.pairs` gives each pair's two lengths, the pairs recognised by name. Report each pair's length difference against the interface's limit, each power net's width and via count against its current, and any rule you could not meet.
