# Xpedition PCB

**Limited prerelease.** Load `../xpedition-cli/SKILL.md` first. If the
entry Skill or matching CLI is unavailable, stop and obtain the matching family;
do not improvise its authorization or recovery procedure. The current candidate
reports `unpublishable`. An older package's native trial does not qualify this one.

## Inspect and plan

Resolve the actual board, its scope, current candidate and owned process. Discover
the exact inspection, placement, routing, check or output leaf through `reference`.
Use complete observations of the actual board, materials, nets, layers and
protected objects. Missing identity, rule provenance, units or contact data stays
unknown. A historical sample, CAD menu or populated command tree does not make a
new board eligible.

For work requiring a closed copy, preserve the real source/backup binding and the
registration produced by the public copy operation. An authorized public startup
may return a post-Open proof for that specific candidate, journal and owned
generation; keep that proof and the original genesis separately. Never fabricate
a proof or use an old copy to bypass the new protocol.

For a design task, use `project inspect --preflight` with actual board
requirements and review the blockers before planning work.

## Bounded component connection

The component-connect route is experimental for this candidate. Discover
`pcb routing trace` and its component query through the live reference. Start
from a public closed-copy registration and its real source backup and origin;
do not substitute a manual copy or invent a receipt.

Use a fresh complete board snapshot and its component/node selectors for the
actual net and signal layer. Plan an explicit simple path and width with no
vias or planes; do not round coordinates to get past the stated grid. The
actual receiver must prove the applicable endpoint, material, clearance,
file, rule and old-copper protections. Incomplete rule sources, local-rule
precedence or unit observations remain unverified; a sampled width is not a
complete effective-rule proof. A same-group no-op is not evidence of a write.

Only an eligible preview may be confirmed once. Keep the original receipt,
then distinguish actual material readback, Save/Close, closed-file verification
and independent reopen. Continue from a prior connection only when the live
contract authenticates the complete prior receipt and required backup. A
partial or unknown result stops the workflow without replay or extra CAD calls.

## Act and verify

STOP CHECKPOINT: use the actual leaf's preview and one matching confirmation
only after all required target, scope, material, file and rule protections pass.
Do not alter protected files, disable checks, relax budgets or substitute another
journal to gain eligibility. Explicit track geometry is not a substitute for
observed endpoint, width and separation evidence.

Report actual execution, material change, Save/Close, closed-file verification,
reopen and independent readback separately. An acknowledgement or image does not
prove persistence, general DRC, rule units, production routing or manufacturing
qualification. Silkscreen remains disabled. Do not automatically run BatchDRC Go
or rerun it to obtain a zero-hazard result.

On partial/unknown or timeout after dispatch, preserve the original operation
and stop further writes, CAD/COM queries, automatic Save/Close/Open, cleanup and
replay. A separate empty-process exit is permitted only when the live contract
provides its complete same-candidate, same-journal, same-generation proof and
the public preview explicitly admits it. Do not borrow another token or resolve
an unknown merely to exit.

## Eval scenarios

- Inspect the current board while preserving all material and state.
- Reject an edit when rule units or protected material cannot be observed.
- Preserve an unknown operation and avoid replay, journal switching and forced exit.
